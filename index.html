About Good News 98

Welcome to Good News 98, where we bring you only the most positive and uplifting news stories in an authentic Windows 98 interface!

Our Mission

In a world full of negative headlines, we believe there's always good news happening. Our platform filters through thousands of news stories to bring you only the positive, inspiring, and uplifting content.

What We Cover

  • Scientific breakthroughs and discoveries
  • Acts of kindness and community support
  • Environmental progress and conservation
  • Technology innovations helping people
  • Arts, culture, and creative achievements
  • Sports accomplishments and inspiring stories
  • Health and wellness breakthroughs
  • Educational advancements

Why Windows 98?

We chose the nostalgic Windows 98 interface because it represents a simpler, more optimistic time in technology. The retro aesthetic serves as a reminder that progress and positivity go hand in hand.
